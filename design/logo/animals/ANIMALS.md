# Planty animal comparison

## How funny it is

| Rank | Animal | Verdict |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Axolotl | The vacant black eyes, permanent smile, and useless little hands make the central joke immediate: no thoughts, only excellent plant care. |
| 2 | Pug | The deranged eyes and loose tongue are extremely funny, although the face is so busy that it competes with the drowning plant. |
| 3 | Toad | The huge grin and garden-native confidence work well, but he looks slightly too capable of understanding cause and effect. |
| 4 | Pigeon | The puffed chest and delighted posture sell unearned confidence, but he reads more like an excited show-off than a truly lovable idiot. |
| 5 | Manatee | The sea-potato premise is strong, but this expression reads as generic party-mascot joy instead of slow, oblivious pride. |

## How well the silhouette survives at 32px

| Rank | Animal | Verdict |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Manatee | The enormous bean-shaped body and tiny flippers remain clean and unmistakably separate from the plant. |
| 2 | Axolotl | The gill crown gives the head a distinctive outline, and the upright body stays readable despite the busy water. |
| 3 | Pigeon | The upright bird shape, beak, legs, and tail survive, although the feather edges start to chatter. |
| 4 | Pug | The compact body survives, but the face that makes the design funny collapses into a noisy cluster of eyes, muzzle, and tongue. |
| 5 | Toad | The squat pose is strong at full size, but the green body merges with the green plant and the silhouette becomes one broad lump. |

## What I would ship

I would ship the **axolotl direction**.
It is the funniest by a clear margin, its empty-headed delight reads before the viewer even notices the flooded pot, and its gills give it enough silhouette identity to survive reduction better than the face-dependent pug.
I would use this illustration as the mascot master, then derive a deliberately simplified small icon from it rather than pretending the full water-and-leaf scene can remain crisp at 32px.

The manatee fails the personality brief even though it wins the silhouette test.
The toad fails the 32px test because animal and foliage fuse together.
The pug is excellent at large sizes but fails as a tiny mark because nearly all of its comedy lives in facial detail.
