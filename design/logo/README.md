# Planty logo

> **Historical exploration.** These are the superseded starfish marks.
> The current mascot is the seal in `design/logo/seal/`; see `design/MASCOT.md`.

Planty is a cheerfully underqualified starfish who beams with pride at the crooked pot and visibly struggling plant in his care.
The plant has a clear stem, two lively leaves, and one drooping leaf so the joke reads without making the mark feel gloomy.

## Files

- `planty-mark.svg` is the primary square colour mark for use at `64 × 64 px` and larger.
- `planty-mark-small.svg` is the simplified colour mark for favicons and other uses from `16 × 16 px` through `63 × 63 px`.
- `planty-mark-mono.svg` is a true one-ink silhouette with transparent detail knockouts, not a greyscale conversion.
- `planty-wordmark-dark.svg` has light letterforms for dark backgrounds.
- `planty-wordmark-light.svg` has dark letterforms for light backgrounds.

Both wordmarks use custom path-based letterforms and have no font dependency.

## Palette

| Role | Dracula Classic colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starfish | Pink | `#ff79c6` |
| Healthy leaves | Green | `#50fa7b` |
| Dark outline and suggested dark background | Background | `#282a36` |
| Light keyline, eyes, and wordmark fill | Foreground | `#f8f8f2` |
| Pot | Orange | `#ffb86c` |
| Pot rim and wilted leaf | Yellow | `#f1fa8c` |

The primary and small colour marks use a light outer keyline around the dark contour.
Choose the wordmark variant for its background; do not rely on the outline to rescue the wrong text colour.
The mono mark uses only `#282a36` and transparency.
Its one ink may be replaced wholesale with any single contrasting colour when a host needs a reversed version.

## Size and clear space

Use `planty-mark-small.svg` below `64 × 64 px`, including at the standard `32 × 32 px` favicon size.
Use `planty-mark.svg` at `64 × 64 px` and larger.
Use the horizontal wordmark at no less than `180 px` wide.
Keep clear space on every side equal to at least one eighth of the mark's displayed width.
Do not crop the pale keyline; it is functional on dark backgrounds.

## Do not

- Do not straighten the pot or revive the yellow leaf, because those two details carry the joke.
- Do not stretch, squash, rotate, mirror, or rearrange the mark.
- Do not substitute the primary mark for the small mark below `64 × 64 px`.
- Do not remove either contour from a colour mark.
- Do not recolour individual pieces outside the documented palette.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, texture, spot patterns, clothing, or extra facial details.
- Do not place the mark over a busy image without a quiet solid-colour container.
- Do not fill the mono knockouts, because they separate the face, plant, and pot in one ink.

## Character distinction

The concept borrows only the broad comic premise of a dim but lovable starfish.
Planty's silhouette, mismatched eye sizes, low pupils, crooked buck-toothed grin, five-arm pose, plant-care prop, clean unspotted surface, palette treatment, and lack of clothing were designed specifically for this project.
The mark does not reproduce the referenced character's facial geometry, body markings, shorts, costume motifs, or exact colour treatment.
